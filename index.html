An IT partner for how your business really runs

TALIA Systems is an Egyptian IT company serving small and medium businesses, retailers and offices. We handle the technology behind the work: the servers, the network, the point-of-sale counter, the backups and the day-to-day support.

We also welcome international clients who need reliable technical delivery. Read about our approach.

What sets our approach apart

  • Open-source first, not open-source only

    We use open tools where they fit your budget and needs, and commercial products where they are the better choice.

  • Built for small and mid-sized organisations

    Recommendations are sized to your real operation, not to an enterprise template.

  • Documented and handed over

    You receive notes on what was built and how it is configured, so you are never dependent on guesswork.

  • Plain-language communication

    Options, costs and risks are explained clearly before work begins.

How we work

  1. Assess

  2. Plan

  3. Implement

  4. Support

    We document, train your team and stay available for maintenance.
